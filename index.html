Innovate 3.0

Hack to Build

All That You Need To Know About Innovate 3.0

Innovate 3.0: Hack to Build is a multi-round innovation challenge where teams will submit ideas, develop prototypes, and present their solutions. With themes spanning Open Innovation, FinTech, Cyber Security, and Sustainability, this event encourages students to create impactful solutions for real-world problems.

Competition Format & Process

Round 1: Idea Submission

Start: 08 Jan 26, 12:00 AM IST
End: 10 Jan 26, 07:00 PM IST

The first milestone of Innovate 3.0: Hack to Build invites teams to submit their project ideas aligned with hackathon themes. With Open Innovation at its core, participants are encouraged to explore diverse technologies and propose creative, impactful solutions to real-world problems.

Hackathon Themes:
Open Innovation FinTech Cyber Security Sustainability

Exceptional ideas from this round will be shortlisted, and selected teams will move on to Round 2.

Round 2: Online Hackathon

Start: 12 Jan 26, 09:00 AM IST
End: 13 Jan 26, 09:01 AM IST

Round 2 is the main 24-hour online hackathon, where shortlisted teams will implement their ideas and build working projects or prototypes. Teams are required to submit a demo video along with repository link of their solution.

This round focuses on execution, functionality, and innovation.

Top-performing teams will be shortlisted and invited to campus for offline panel evaluation and prize distribution.

Note: This is an elimination round.

Result Declaration

14 Jan 26, 07:15 PM IST

Results of Demo/Hackathon Round will be announced. Shortlisted teams will qualify for Final Offline Round.

Final Round

Start: 17 Jan 26, 09:17 AM IST
End: 17 Jan 26, 07:18 PM IST

Selected teams from hackathon will be invited to college campus for final presentation of their projects. The presentations will be evaluated by a judging panel, and winners will be announced on the same day.

Guidelines

All students from any undergraduate stream are eligible to participate.

Inter-college teams are allowed.

Inter-specialization teams are allowed.

The event includes a 24-hour online hackathon, which will begin after Idea Submission round.

After hackathon, shortlisted teams will be invited to college campus for offline presentation, panel evaluation, and winner announcement.

Duration of Rounds

Round 1: Online Idea Submission
Round 2: 24-hour Online Hackathon (Demo Round)
Final Round: Offline presentation and evaluation on campus

Team Formation

Teams can consist of a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 4 members.

Inter-college teams are allowed.

Inter-specialization teams are allowed.

Rules & Regulations

  • Hackathon themes include Open Innovation, FinTech, Cyber Security, and Sustainability.
  • College ID is mandatory for participation.
  • On the day of offline evaluation, each team will be allotted 4–5 minutes to present their project.
  • Special prizes will be awarded to category/theme winners.
  • The decision of the judging panel will be final and binding.

Prizes & Rewards

The total prize pool is ₹50,000+

Special prizes will be awarded to category/theme winners.
