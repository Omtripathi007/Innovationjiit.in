The Challenge

Delhi-NCR remains among the world's most polluted regions, with air quality reaching hazardous levels during certain times of the year. This hackathon challenges students to leverage data and technology to create community-scale solutions to monitor, predict, and reduce air pollution in the region.

Focus Areas

Participants are encouraged to develop innovative solutions that address various aspects of air pollution in Delhi-NCR. Your solutions should leverage data analysis, artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies to create impactful interventions.

Potential Solution Areas:
Air Quality Monitoring Pollution Prediction Models Source Identification Health Impact Assessment Public Awareness Tools Policy Recommendation Systems

SIH Internal Hackathon

Smart India Hackathon — College Internals at JIIT 128

Aug 04–15, 2026 National Innovation Challenge

Build solutions that matter for India

Smart India Hackathon is a premier nationwide initiative that brings together students, institutions, mentors, and government departments to solve real-world challenges with technology. The JIIT 128 internal round is designed to help students experience the SIH spirit early, collaborate in teams, and develop ideas that can create meaningful impact.

Register Now Know More on Govt Website
01

What is SIH?

Smart India Hackathon is a flagship national innovation movement that encourages students to solve live challenges from industry and government departments through practical, scalable ideas.

  • Nationwide innovation challenge
  • Problem-solving with real impact
  • Mentorship and industry exposure
02

Why Participate?

Our internal SIH round helps students sharpen ideas, improve pitching, and experience the full innovation cycle before they compete on a larger stage.

  • Team-based problem solving
  • Mentor guidance and feedback
  • Chance to represent JIIT nationally
03

Themes You Can Explore

The SIH journey is built around themes such as robotics and drones, clean and green technology, tourism, and other civic-tech challenges that matter to India.

  • Robotics and Drones
  • Clean & Green Technology
  • Tourism and Social Impact

How the journey works

From campus ideation to national-level innovation, the SIH experience is built to nurture ideas into practical solutions.

Campus Ideation

Students identify a real problem statement, form teams, and begin refining ideas around feasibility, urgency, and innovation.

Prototype & Build

Teams work on a prototype, build a working solution, and get support from mentors, faculty, and innovation communities.

Showcase & Scale

The strongest ideas move forward to represent JIIT at the larger Smart India Hackathon ecosystem with measurable impact.
